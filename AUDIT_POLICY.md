Audit policy: every finding must be triaged within 5 business days.

## Policy details

**Policy owner:** Hezi Liu

Triage of findings is tracked in the "Security audit readiness checklist" issue (#1) in this repository, with each area of coverage attached as a sub-issue:

- #2 Dependabot alert review
- #3 Secret scanning review
- #4 Code scanning coverage

All security findings — including Dependabot alerts, secret scanning alerts, and code scanning alerts — must be reviewed and triaged (assessed, assigned, and dispositioned) within 5 business days of detection. Triage outcomes and evidence are recorded against the corresponding sub-issue in the checklist.
